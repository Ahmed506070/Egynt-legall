Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Egynt Chatbot ("we", "us") is an educational chatbot available through Instagram messaging. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Your Instagram username and the ID Meta assigns to your account.
  • The messages you send to the chatbot.

How We Use Your Information

  • To reply to your messages and provide the chatbot service.
  • To improve the quality of the educational content.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers needed to run the chatbot (such as Meta and our hosting providers), or when required by law.

Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see our Data Deletion page).

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.
